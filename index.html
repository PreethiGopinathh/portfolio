I like building technology that starts with understanding people.

I work at the intersection of cloud systems, data visualization, LLMs, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), turning complex information into clarity worth acting on. I learn by building: Sentinel brings video analytics and location-aware reporting to urban safety, and ArthaMind, an agentic AI project, helps people manage their finances with confidence. Each starts with a real human need, and the technology follows.

Alongside my technical work, I have developed strong leadership and management skills through my college’s E-Cell. I progressed from event organizer to Director of Event Planning and currently serve as Chief Executive Officer, leading teams, managing logistics and budgets, and driving initiatives that encourage student entrepreneurship. As the Event Lead for TITANIUM, a three-day national-level technical festival hosted by Rajalakshmi Engineering College, I coordinated and managed 75+ events. As a member of UiPhoria’s editorial team, I contributed to content and program planning around automation and agentic AI, including UiPhoria Academic Summit 3.0.

I am driven by continuous growth and a commitment to helping the communities I belong to grow with me. At the core of my work is curiosity about people, their perspectives and needs, and a belief that technology should respond to them thoughtfully.
